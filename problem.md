\# Problem



Students and people who work with computers or phones often need to write down a quick thought/reminder or other pieces of information to find it again later. Real life paper can get lost, and other applications such as word processors are heavy for short notes, and they often need an account or a setup. These people need a fast and private place to store notes.



\# Initial requirements



\- R1. The user can type a note and save it

\- R2. Saved notes appear in a list

\- R3. User can open a saved note and read it

\- R4. Saved notes are still there after the browser is closed and reopened

\- R5. User can edit a saved note

\- R6. User can delete a note

\- R7. No account or login is needed to use the app
